# Living Requirements

A manifesto for building products with AI.

**Code is cheap now. Knowing what to build, and knowing it still does what we intended, is not.**

We believe:

## 1. Discover, don't assume

Requirements emerge through describing, prototyping, trying, and learning. We don't pretend to know everything upfront.

## 2. Think big. Act small

Hold on to the vision, but take the smallest step a real person can try. Build the simplest solution that proves the idea.

## 3. Preserve what we learn

What matters becomes a living rule with proof. Our understanding evolves, and our requirements evolve with it.

## 4. Keep the promises. Replace the implementation

Rules describe what the product promises, not how its code works. Implementation can change freely as long as those promises remain true.

## 5. Humans own the intent. AI helps maintain alignment

Humans decide what matters, what the product should promise, and remain accountable for it. AI helps discover, express, challenge, prove, implement, and keep it all in sync.

## 6. Build for real people. Generalize from reality

Solve problems for people we can reach. Discover what is shared through experience rather than designing for imaginary future needs.

## 7. Proof is not success

Proof tells us whether we built what we intended. Real use tells us whether that intention was right.

## 8. Keep the method alive and the truth portable

Improve how we work through experience. Keep product knowledge understandable and usable without depending on any particular tool.

## Our bet

AI can help keep intent, rules, proof, and code aligned for as long as a product lives.

**We test this. We do not assume it.**
